# Aggressive xG Wave G3

G1 and G2 showed that Strict's forced-shot substrate could not be converted into
reliable close chances against G4. G3 therefore uses Nova Vertical—the strongest
repeatable chance producer—as its executable parent.

The crossover adds:

- phase-dependent forward lanes: wide during buildup, one inner-channel runner in
  the final third;
- Strict Switch's early far-side displacement;
- Release and Run's passer-goes-beyond behavior;
- an advanced player 2 as bounce option and first counterpresser;
- a compulsory clear finish inside 22 metres, while retaining Nova's choices
  outside that high-value zone.
