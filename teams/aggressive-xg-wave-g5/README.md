# Aggressive xG Wave G5

G4's first side-swapped screen against Release Deep Denial G4 produced three
shots in two matches, including two from 18–19 metres. That exceeded Nova
Vertical's historical total-shot and close-shot rates, but the reverse leg also
contained one 29 m attempt and one harmful MOVE_TO failure.

G5 removes optional shots beyond 24 metres and recovers exhausted off-ball
MOVE_TO failures through the existing semantic validator. It is the first
candidate intended for broader paired validation.

## Initial validation

Four clean matches produced a 1-3-0 record, 1:0 goals, and four shots—all from
within 22 metres (15.0, 15.5, 17.9, and 21.4 m; mean 17.4 m).

- Against Release Deep Denial G4: two 0-0 draws; G5 created two close shots and
  G4 created one.
- Against Nova Vertical: 1-0 aggregate; both teams created two shots, but G5's
  averaged 18.2 m versus Nova's 23.5 m and G5 created the only goal.

This is promising xG-proxy evidence, not a statistically calibrated xG estimate.
