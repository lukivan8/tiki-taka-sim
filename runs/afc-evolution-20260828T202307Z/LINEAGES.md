# Evolutionary Lineages

## Release and Run lineage

Seed: `vertical-wingbacks` → existing child `release-and-run`.

The parent/child comparison with `vertical-wingbacks` was inconclusive.

## Final lineage selection

- Release leader transition: `release-and-run` → `release-deep-denial-g4`. G2/G3 were reliability/shape steps; G4 earned broad and held-out evidence.
- Champion attack: `strict-wide-pocket-g3` → `strict-switch-counter-g4` exposed G4 with a repeatable four-shot paired advantage.
- Robustification: G4 + exploiter evidence → `release-switch-shield-g5`; the targeted repair succeeded but did not generalize against Simple.
- Final frozen validation: G4–G5 0-0 aggregate; both held Nova 0-0/0-0; Simple beat G4 1-0 in its home orientation; G4–Strict Switch split 1-1. Retain G4 as stable evolved primary, G5 as targeted alternative, Strict Switch as provisional counter, and Simple as provisional Elo-leading backup/control.

Known unresolved mechanism: the decisive Simple goals against both Release finalists occurred when Simple was home, including a tick-5 final shot against G4 and a tick-56 late shot against G5. Side orientation, kickoff ownership, formation mirroring, or a Simple-specific transition is implicated; evidence does not isolate which. This is a held-out strategy/representation question for morning calibration, not grounds for an unvalidated mutation after freeze.
